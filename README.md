# Sounak Senapati

### A lawyer who likes building things.

I'm a lawyer by education, an operator by experience, and a curious builder by instinct.

My interests sit at the intersection of **AI, engineering, robotics, law, and entrepreneurship**. I've spent much of my professional life working around businesses, products, strategy, and operations. Over time, I've become just as interested in the systems and technology that make those businesses work.

I'm not a traditional software engineer. I enjoy understanding how things work, finding problems worth solving, and using technology to experiment with possible solutions.

This GitHub is my public workshop: a place to document ideas, prototypes, experiments, and the things I'm learning along the way.

## What I'm exploring

- **AI & intelligent systems** — AI-powered tools, agents, retrieval, memory, and workflow automation.
- **Engineering & robotics** — The intersection of software, hardware, CAD, firmware, manufacturing, and physical systems.
- **Legal technology** — Better ways to approach legal workflows, documents, research, and compliance.
- **Product & operations** — Tools and systems that help people and businesses work better.
- **Entrepreneurship** — Understanding real-world problems and learning how to turn useful ideas into products.

## The bigger ambition

One day, I want to build something of my own: a meaningful product or company that solves a difficult, real-world problem.

I'm particularly fascinated by the intersection of AI, robotics, industrial systems, engineering, and infrastructure. I don't have the whole map figured out. The projects here are part of the process: learning, testing ideas, breaking things, rebuilding them, and getting better with each iteration.

## Building in public

I believe ideas improve when people can inspect them, question them, and build on them.

The repositories here are shared as a record of experimentation and learning, not as a claim that every project is complete or production-ready. Please check each project's README and licence before using or building on it.

If something interests you, feel free to explore, fork, contribute, or get in touch.

**Still learning. Still building. Hopefully, building something that matters.**
